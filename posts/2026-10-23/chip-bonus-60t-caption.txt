⠀
한국은행이 22일 낸 보고서에 따르면
삼성전자·SK하이닉스가 내년 1분기 약 60조 원의 성과급을 줄 전망이에요.

🇺🇸 Samsung and SK Hynix bonuses could hit ₩60 trillion.
⠀
In a report on Oct 22, the Bank of Korea estimated that
Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix will pay about ₩60 trillion in bonuses in Q1 2027.
Across major chip, auto and shipbuilding firms: ₩60–70 trillion, about 6x this year.
⠀
The BOK expects this to lift 2027 private consumption growth by 0.1–0.15 points,
but the gains go mostly to high earners and may take time to spread.
